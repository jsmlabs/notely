# Notely — approved brand asset manifest v2.0.8

## Canonical identity
- `mark.svg` — canonical brand master, 128×128 viewBox.
- `mark-light.svg`, `mark-transparent.svg` — contrast and placement variants.
- `mark-monochrome-light.svg`, `mark-monochrome-dark.svg` — monochrome uses only; verify contrast before use.
- `wordmark-light.svg`, `wordmark-dark.svg` — prior supported horizontal lockups.
- `lockup-on-dark.svg`, `lockup-on-light.svg` — 600×160 branded signatures.
- `mark-{16,32,48,128,256,512,1024}.png` — exports from the same vector master.
- `../icons/icon{16,32,48,128}.png` — manifest-linked extension icons.
- `../store-assets/` — draft promotional assets, not runtime screenshots.

All artwork is local and self-contained. Preserve 1:1 aspect for icons, do not stretch, and leave a minimum clear space of 12.5% of icon width.
